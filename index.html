What is analytics data reconciliation?

Analytics data reconciliation means comparing the same business events — orders, leads, payments — across every system that records them, record by record. Your CRM or store backend is the source of truth; GA4, ad platforms, your data warehouse and dashboards are checked against it.

The result is a list of concrete discrepancies: which orders never reached analytics, which were counted twice, where the traffic source was lost, and which dashboard numbers don't match the data behind them.

What we find

The usual reasons your reports don't match the money in the bank.

Orders lost on the way to analytics
We match by order ID and show where the loss happens: web or app, which page, which traffic source.
Duplicate purchases
Purchase events re-sent on thank-you page reloads, two GA4 tags firing at once, double loads into the warehouse.
Conversions credited to the wrong channel
Clicks with a gclid recorded as direct traffic, or your payment provider showing up as the referral source.
Leads that never reach the CRM
The form was submitted but no lead was created — or it was, without UTM parameters.
Warehouses and dashboards that disagree
Days and rows dropped between layers, incomplete marts, dashboard numbers that don't match the data behind them.
Broken tracking on the website
Double page_view, tags firing before cookie consent, personal data in requests, dataLayer events with no tag attached.

Frequently asked questions

What access do you need?

Read-only access only: viewer role in GA4 or BigQuery, a read-only CRM user, a warehouse user with SELECT on the tables we check. If you can't grant access, we work from exports in any format: CSV, Excel, JSON.

What happens to personal data?

It never reaches reports or normalized tables: emails and phone numbers are replaced with one-way hashes. Records can still be matched across systems by hash, but the original data cannot be restored.

How is this different from GA4's built-in diagnostics?

GA4 diagnostics only look at GA4. We compare GA4 with your CRM, warehouse, dashboards and what your website actually sends, order by order, and check your project's own business rules.

Can agencies white-label the reports?

Yes. PDF reports carry your agency name, contact and brand color. Monitoring can be added one client project at a time.

Which tools do you support?

GA4 and BigQuery exports, Google Ads and Meta, HubSpot, Salesforce, Shopify, WooCommerce, custom CRMs, PostgreSQL, MySQL, ClickHouse, Snowflake, Looker Studio, Power BI, Metabase, Google Tag Manager and in-house trackers. Anything that can be exported or queried by SQL or API can be reconciled.

How much does an analytics audit cost?

From $100 to $600 depending on data volume and the number of systems. Ongoing monitoring costs $20 per month per report or dashboard, or $150 per month for a whole project.
